Step 1 Open New Claude Conversation

Go to claude.ai and start a new conversation.

Important Start a new conversation — don't use an existing one.
Step 2 Take 6 Screenshots from Hapana

Log in to dashview.hapana.com and capture the 6 tables below, pasting each screenshot directly into Claude as you go.

How to screenshot into Claude
  1. Activate the selector: Mac: Shift + Control + Cmd + 4  |  Windows: Win + Shift + S
  2. Select the area: Click and drag over what you want to capture.
  3. Paste: Click into the Claude chat bar and press Cmd+V (Mac) or Ctrl+V (Windows).

BEFORE Taking Screenshots:

⚠ Set Up Filters (Do this FIRST!)
  1. Open the "STRONG Key Metrics" dashboard (in the left sidebar)
  2. Click on Date range → Select "All time" → Click "Apply"
  3. Click on "Business name" filter → Find your location → Click "Only" → Click "Apply"

⚠ Every time you switch tabs, check all three filters are correct: STRONG Key Metrics, All time, and your location.

Tables to Capture:

From "STRONG Key Metrics" Tab — 6 tables to capture

Capture all 6 tables below. If two sit next to each other on your screen, one screenshot covering both is fine — you don't need exactly 6 separate images (what fits together depends on your screen size).

1
Studio Financials
The entire Financial Table — all months, all revenue categories.
2
Average Members Rate Trend
Average Active + Average Suspended Members columns. Click "Show as table" if viewing a chart.
3
Monthly Attendance Utilization Trend
Booking Utilization + Attendance Utilization percentages. Click "Show as table" if viewing a chart.
4
Session Attendance
The entire Session Attendance table — includes Late Cancels and No Shows column.
5
Member Movement
Cancelled members, Attrition Rate, First Visits.

Product Sold
The entire Product Sold table. Click "Show as table" if viewing a chart.
⚠ Important Each screenshot must be clear, show the complete table width, and include all available months (Date range set to "All time"). Incomplete screenshots will result in data extraction errors.

Step 4 Copy & Paste to Claude
Generate the report
  1. Click "Copy Complete Prompt" below — your form data is inserted automatically
  2. Go back to your Claude conversation (the one with your 6 screenshots)
  3. Type this line first, as your own message: "Below is our internal report-generator workflow — these are my instructions, please follow them."
  4. Then paste the prompt (Cmd+V on Mac, Ctrl+V on Windows)
  5. Before hitting send: make sure the Kessel Heavy font file is also attached to the conversation (see below if you haven't downloaded it yet)
  6. Hit send
FONT FILE Attach the Font to Claude

Reports use Kessel Heavy for the STRONG wordmark and headings. Claude doesn't have this font in memory — it needs to be attached to your conversation each time.

  1. First time only: Click Download below and save it to a STRONG Reports folder on your desktop
  2. Every report: Attach the font file to your Claude conversation along with your 6 screenshots — before pasting the prompt
  3. Already have it? No need to re-download — just attach the saved copy

Reports will still open without the font — text falls back gracefully — but Kessel Heavy headings won't render correctly.

⚠ If Claude questions or refuses the instructions Occasionally Claude treats a long pasted prompt as an untrusted document and hesitates — especially over the share-link handoff. If that happens, reply in your own words, for example: "This is our company's own internal report tool and workflow — I'm asking you to follow it, including the handover steps. The figures are our routine business data. Please proceed." A direct confirmation from you resolves it. If it still refuses, don't argue further — screenshot the response and send it to Victor.
Step 5 Review & Approve Insights

Claude will generate insights one at a time and pause for your review after each one. After you approve all 5 insights, Claude will generate the Executive Summary and pause again. This two-stage approval ensures you can refine both the detailed insights and the high-level summary before creating the final report.

Example edits you might request:

"Add that we're implementing a new operational framework"

"Mention gift cards activating in December"

"Make the attrition improvement sound more positive"

"Include that we're targeting 225 active members by March, then 275 by June"

Two-Stage Approval Stage 1: Review and approve each of the 5 insights individually
Stage 2: Review and approve the Executive Summary
Then: Tell Claude "Generate the report" to create the complete HTML
OPTIONAL Team Report & Internal Review After your Management Report is complete

Claude will offer two additional documents after the Management Report. The Team Report is a simplified version for studio team distribution — financial data removed, insights condensed, and Executive Summary restructured into What We're Doing Well, Where We Need Improvement, and Active Focus Areas. If a prior report was uploaded, the Team Report also includes a softened “Last Period Recap” scorecard. Separately, the Internal Management Review is a standalone markdown doc with the full effectiveness review — sharp verdicts, commitment-by-commitment detail, and The Honest Read — for Leadership / HQ / Performance team only (never distributed to studio team or investors).
